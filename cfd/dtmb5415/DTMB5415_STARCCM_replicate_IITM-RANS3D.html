Tags: RANS3D = value from your IITM-RANS3D run. ASSUMED = my closest reasonable choice until you give the RANS3D value. Replace every ASSUMED value for a true replication; the list at the end says which ones matter most.

What "replicate" can mean here. STAR-CCM+ uses an unstructured trimmed/overset mesh with VOF. If RANS3D uses a structured or body-fitted grid, or a different free-surface method, the cells can't be identical. What can be matched is the cell size in each region, the extents of those regions, the near-wall resolution, the time step, the physics and the motion. This sheet matches those.

  • Overset matching: the background refined region must use exactly 30 × 30 × 10 mm where it overlaps the overset region. Keep at least 4 cells of overlap and at least 4 cells between the prism-layer top and the overset boundary. With 30 mm cells the region needs ≈ 0.12 m clearance, which the 0.2 m side margin gives.
  • Cells per transverse Kelvin wavelength (2.656 m): 89. Aspect ratio Δx/Δz = 3.
  • VOF with 10 mm vertical cells: HRIC smears the interface over 2–3 cells (20–30 mm). To limit this, set the VOF sharpening factor to 0.1 and keep the free-surface Courant number ≤ 0.5 (see time step). Expect the wave crests right at the hull to be slightly lower than on a finer STAR grid. This is part of replicating the RANS3D resolution, not an error.

Residual convergence target: 1e-4

Every time step iterates until all residuals below reach 1e-4, with a cap on inner iterations so a step that stalls doesn't run forever.

  • Check the residual plot after about 2 L/U. If continuity or the volume fraction stalls around 1e-3 on most steps and the cap of 20 is hit every time, the time step is too large for this grid. Reduce Δt, for example from 0.005 to 0.0035 L/U, rather than raising the cap.
  • In unsteady VOF runs with DFBI, continuity is usually the last residual to reach 1e-4, especially just after the body release and during the ramp. Some steps hitting the cap during 1–4 s is normal.
  • The residual target is not the resistance convergence test. Also require RT, sinkage and trim to change by less than 0.5–1% over the last 5–10 L/U.

4. Comparison to report

CT, with the pressure and friction parts separated; sinkage and trim; wave cuts at y/L = 0.082, 0.172 and 0.324 (the INSEAN and IIHR positions); and the wave pattern contour. Use the same non-dimensionalisation (S at static draft, ρ, V) in both solvers. A difference in the friction part points to wall treatment or turbulence model; a difference in the pressure part points to free-surface resolution or method.

5. RANS3D values that decide how exact the match is

  1. Motion: free sinkage and trim, or fixed? If fixed, at what attitude?
  2. Refined-region extents: length ahead/behind, width and vertical band of the 30 × 10 mm region.
  3. Near-wall grid: first-cell height or y+, wall functions or resolved, and hull surface spacing.
  4. Turbulence model and free-surface method (level set or VOF).
  5. Time step and physical time run.
  6. Domain extents and total cell count.
  7. Results (CT, sinkage, trim) to compare against.
